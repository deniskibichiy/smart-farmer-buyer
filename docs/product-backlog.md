
# Product Backlog — Smart Farmer-to-Buyer Management System

## 1. Product Goal

To develop a platform that connects farmers with suitable buyers of agricultural produce by considering factors such as crop type, quantity, location, price, and availability.

The system aims to make it easier for farmers to market their produce and for buyers to find suitable suppliers.

## 3. First Increment: Registration and Authentication

### Objective
Establish the user account and authentication functionality required before users can access personalised platform features.

### Initial Backlog Items

| ID | Requirement | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| PB-01 | User registration | Users can create accounts using valid details. |
| PB-02 | User login and logout | Registered users can access and exit their accounts. |
| PB-03 | Authentication validation and access control | Invalid credentials are rejected, and access is controlled according to the implemented requirements. |

### Increment Outcome

The first increment focuses on registration and authentication. These capabilities establish the foundation for subsequent features, including produce listing management and buyer access to the marketplace.

**Implementation status:** Confirm the completed functionality against the actual codebase before marking each item as Done.

## 4. Planned Future Increments

- **Increment 2:** Produce listing creation, listing management, and marketplace search.
- **Increment 3:** Farmer-buyer matching and comparison of suitable suppliers.
- **Later increments:** Communication, ordering, order tracking, and additional marketplace capabilities.
- **Future enhancements:** Demand prediction, advanced analytics, and transport optimisation.

These are initial planning assumptions and may change as the team receives feedback and learns more during development.

## 5. Product Prioritisation Rationale

The backlog is initially ordered according to user value, dependencies, feasibility, and the product's core objective.

1. **Registration and authentication** provide the foundation for identifying users and controlling access.
2. **Produce listings and marketplace search** establish the supply and discovery functions of the platform.
3. **Farmer-buyer matching** builds on the availability of produce and buyer requirements to help users identify suitable trading partners.
4. **Communication and order tracking** support coordination after a suitable match has been found.
5. **Advanced analytics and optimisation** can be considered after the core marketplace functionality is established.

The Product Owner maintains and orders the Product Backlog as requirements and priorities evolve. The Developers contribute technical estimates, identify dependencies, and determine how selected work can be delivered.

## 6. Backlog Management

- Backlog items should have clear descriptions and acceptance criteria before development.
- Priorities should be reviewed as user needs, technical constraints, and stakeholder feedback change.
- The team selects work for each Sprint during Sprint Planning.
- The Sprint Backlog contains the selected work and the team's plan for delivering the Sprint Goal.
- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests provide traceable evidence of task assignment, implementation, review, and integration.
- An item should be marked Done only when it meets the team's agreed Definition of Done.
